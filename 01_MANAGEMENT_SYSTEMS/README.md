# Layer 01 — Management Systems

**EATGF Layer:** 01_MANAGEMENT_SYSTEMS
**Governance Scope:** Architecture | Policy
**Status:** Under Active Development — v1.1-Enhanced (Q3 2026)

---

## Layer Purpose

The Management Systems layer defines the formal, standards-aligned management frameworks that govern how EATGF controls are implemented and maintained across an organisation. It provides the procedural backbone for both Information Security Management (ISMS) and AI Management (AIMS), ensuring governance is systematic, auditable, and demonstrably controlled.

This layer translates strategic governance intent (established in Layer 00 — Foundation) into repeatable, audit-ready management processes.

---

## Scope

| System                                        | Standard Alignment               | Scope                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Information Security Management System (ISMS) | ISO/IEC 27001:2022               | Organisation-wide information security controls and risk management |
| AI Management System (AIMS)                   | ISO/IEC 42001:2023               | AI system lifecycle governance, risk, and accountability            |
| Risk Management Framework                     | ISO 31000:2018                   | Enterprise-wide risk identification, assessment, and treatment      |
| Supplier and Third-Party Management           | ISO 27001:2022 Annex A.5.19–5.22 | Vendor risk governance and contractual security requirements        |
