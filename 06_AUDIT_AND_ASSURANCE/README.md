# Layer 06 — Audit and Assurance

**EATGF Layer:** 06_AUDIT_AND_ASSURANCE
**Governance Scope:** Audit Methodology | Implementation Standard
**Status:** Partially Published — Assurance Standards v1.0 Available

---

## Layer Purpose

The Audit and Assurance layer defines the methodology, standards, and procedures for conducting internal governance audits, managing corrective actions, and preparing for external certification. It is the operational assurance backbone of EATGF, ensuring that governance controls are not merely documented but demonstrably operating and continuously improving.

This layer is aligned to ISO 19011:2018 (Guidelines for Auditing Management Systems).

## Control Authority Relationship

This layer **references controls** from all other layers and **produces audit evidence** that validates control effectiveness. Audit findings feed back into Layer 02 (Control Architecture) for control refinement and Layer 03 (Governance Models) for maturity reassessment.
